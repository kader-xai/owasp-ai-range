Learning brief · architecture, objective, and limitations

The model ball receives records selected by the application. The weak lookup puts Bob’s record inside Alice’s ball; the defended lookup checks ownership before the record reaches it.

Offline mode serializes actual retrieved records; it is not a model. Ollama receives exactly those records and may refuse, omit, or hallucinate information. The lab separately records unauthorized retrieval and private-field disclosure. A refusal does not repair a broken repository boundary.

New workspaces provision a demo Alice identity, not production authentication. Data and attempts persist only if RANGE_DB points to a writable SQLite file. No real accounts, messages, or customer data.
